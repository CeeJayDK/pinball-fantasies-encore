HIGH SCORES

You get a high score locally, the game asks SEND ONLINE (Y OR N) after your initials. A yes sends a recording of the game; the server replays it before your score appears here.

Each score shows the initials typed for it and unique tag of your game. Download a recording (RPL) and drop it on the game to watch it.

A native remake of Pinball Fantasies, the 1994 PC release, for macOS, Windows and Linux.

It reads the original game's own files and runs the physics, the table rules, the dot matrix and the music itself: nothing is emulated. All four tables play exactly as the original does, under either the 1994 pictures or remastered ones.

  • The original rules and physics, taken from the game's own data.
  • Remastered artwork, every table redrawn at high resolution, not upscaled, with its lamps still working. F10 switches between it and the original pixels at any time.
  • A CRT look with scanlines and glow, on F9.
  • Online high scores, every one replayed by the server from a recording of the game before it counts.
  • Free and open: the code under GPL-3.0, the new artwork under CC BY-SA 4.0.
